The brand

A defined focus.

Maison Cedro's scope is limited to actually aged cigars and specially finished cigars.

Aging and finishing are separate descriptions. The brand's standard is to distinguish documented time from any additional finishing process, without implying an age, origin or history that has not been established.

Actual aging.

Use aging descriptions only where actual aging is supported by records. Do not substitute an aesthetic or a finishing description for elapsed time.

Distinct finishing.

Describe special finishing separately from aging. A finishing process does not, by itself, establish how long a cigar has been aged.

Recorded provenance.

Keep source, identity and available aging or finishing records connected to the relevant description. State only what the documentation supports.
