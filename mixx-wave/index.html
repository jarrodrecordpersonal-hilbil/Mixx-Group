Before the first screen

Good questions.

A few things to sort out before bringing any new platform into a working venue.

Does this replace everything already on my TVs?

The programming plan should work around your venue. A pilot can be scoped alongside your existing schedule; switching, routing and content rights need to be confirmed for the setup.

What equipment do we need?

Screen, player, network, audio and staff-control requirements are checked for each pilot. This page does not promise compatibility with every television or streaming device.

Where does the programming come from?

MIXX TANK is the group’s original-programming studio. The actual content lineup and any additional creator or partner rights are agreed for the venue.

Is this the same thing as SWAY IRL?

No. MIXXWAVE is the screen and venue programming direction. SWAY IRL focuses on in-store media and measurement. Explore SWAY IRL →
