# Sunday Pours — brand and launch-page direction

## Approved direction
Name: SUNDAY POURS. The former Sunday Poors spelling and financial joke are retired.
Master line: Golf. Good company. Great pours.
Positioning: a broad-appeal golf-and-lifestyle entertainment brand, with the show and personalities first. Selected drinks, apparel and experiences extend the brand; alcohol purchase or consumption is not required to enjoy the show.
Brand relationship: MIXX GROUP brand; MIXX TANK programming.

## Creative system
Primary color: Clubhouse green #214D3D.
Background: Warm cream #F6F0E3.
Accent: Flag orange #DD623E.
Secondary: Sage #DEE5D6 and sun gold #E7B45D.
Serif wordmark and headlines, straightforward sans-serif body text. The small emblem combines a course flag, green/horizon, and sun. Use the emblem for small applications; keep the full name readable on web, apparel and partner materials.

## Site
Route: /sunday-pours/.
Linked from MIXX GROUP and the MIXX TANK programming slate.
Dependency-free static HTML/CSS/JS; local SVG assets. No third-party forms, tracking scripts, checkout, fake sales counters or autoplay video.
Mobile menu and accessible chapter tabs support keyboard navigation. Reduced-motion preference respected.

## Source and limits
Adapted from Jarrod's approved direction and MixxTank Q4 Marketing Plan: pour-by-hole studio show, broader audience bridge, golf merchandise and travel/lifestyle direction.
The three chapter descriptions are new editorial proposals, not transcripts or claims about a released episode.
The show has no claimed public release date. Apparel illustrations are explicitly concepts and not for sale. No bottle pricing, low-proof claims or product availability is implied.
The existing MIXX TANK YouTube destination is retained from the current repository; it is not presented as an already-released Sunday Pours episode.
Trademark clearance, approved production artwork, final merchandise specifications and rights review remain separate launch tasks.
