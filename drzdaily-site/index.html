04 / Ingredient philosophy

Interesting science.
No miracle promises.

Our proposed formula combines ingredients with established nutritional roles and compounds that are still being studied. We believe the difference matters.

01

Foundations first.

Creatine is well studied for training performance. Omega-3s are dietary fats. Glycine, taurine and magnesium have distinct nutritional roles. No ingredient replaces eating well, movement or good sleep.

02

Explore what comes next.

Urolithin A, NMN, ergothioneine, Ca-AKG and other specialty compounds are active research areas. Their presence in a formula is not evidence they extend human lifespan.

03

Quality over hype.

Ingredient doses, safety, supplier specifications and product claims still need review before launch. We'll disclose material formulation changes before they reach subscribers.

We are developing a supplement—not a medical treatment. Some ingredients may be inappropriate with certain conditions or medications. This is a prelaunch concept, not a recommendation to combine these ingredients yourself.

07 / Good questions

A few things worth knowing.

Clear expectations are part of the product.

Is the monthly price $199?

Our current target is $199 for a 30-day recurring subscription. This is a prelaunch offer concept, not a current sale. We'll confirm the final price once the formula, manufacturing and packaging are ready.

How much does it cost per day?

At the proposed $199 every 30 days, the full AM / PM / BEDTIME routine works out to about $6.63 per day. The formula and pricing are still under review, and checkout is not open yet.

Is this 90 days of supplements?

No. Each monthly box is a 30-day supply, with 90 individually portioned packs: one AM, one PM and one bedtime pack per day.

Does the formula prove that I will live longer?

No. Longevity science is evolving. Some ingredients are better studied than others, and none of the proposed 18-ingredient combination has been shown to extend human lifespan. We will continue to review the science and avoid miracle claims.

Do I need to be on TRT?

No. TRT is not part of the DRZ Daily supplement pack, and medical treatment is not required. Anyone considering TRT should speak with a licensed clinician who can evaluate symptoms, testing and risks.

Why does the formula include some emerging ingredients?

The formula is inspired by a personal longevity routine and includes ingredients with different levels of scientific evidence. Some have well-established nutritional or exercise-related roles; others are preliminary areas of research. We will only finalize ingredients and permissible claims after appropriate expert review.

Will the ingredients ever change?

Possibly. We plan to continually assess evidence, safety and quality. If we make a material change, we'll explain what changed, why it changed and what it means before sending members an updated formula.

Can I pause or cancel?

Our planned subscription includes pause, skip and cancellation options without a long-term contract. The final terms will be published when checkout launches.

Can I use the supplements with my medications?

Not everyone should use every ingredient. Supplements can interact with medications and medical conditions, so the final formula will require safety review and consumers should discuss personal suitability with their clinicians.
